# 0.1.0

Initial release:

- Compile-time StaticMonth grids, masks, and weekday summaries.
- Runtime MonthGrid with fixed six-week and natural layouts.
- Gregorian CivilDate, YearMonth, weekday, leap-year, and ordinal-day helpers.
- Relation, weekday, weekend, and current-month masks.
- Fixed-storage cell and week views.
- Dated month topology and genuinely undated blank-grid support.
- Structural calendar fingerprints and a compact StaticYear summary.
